Glideshot: screen recordings, shot like film
============================================

A Mac screen recorder with a virtual camera that tilts, zooms and follows your cursor.
You can change the angle, focus and blur after recording.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
Website and full install guide: https://glideshot.xyz/install/


INSTALL (about a minute, only once)
-------
1. Drag Glideshot.app into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click Glideshot. macOS says it can't verify the app, because Glideshot isn't
   notarized by Apple. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
3. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Glideshot. Confirm with your password.
   The button is there for about an hour after step 2.
   (macOS 14 Sonoma: instead of steps 2-3, Control-click Glideshot, choose Open, click Open.)
4. Allow Screen Recording when asked (System Settings -> Privacy & Security ->
   Screen & System Audio Recording -> Glideshot), then quit Glideshot (Cmd+Q) and open it again.

Prefer Terminal? This lets macOS open it without asking:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glideshot.app

"Glideshot is damaged and can't be opened"? Run the Terminal command above.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Frame the shot in the main window, then press Record (or Ctrl+Option+Cmd+R).
2. Press Ctrl+Option+Cmd+R again to stop. The take opens in the editor.
3. Change the angle, zoom, blur, camera behavior and cursor while you scrub or play (Space),
   pick formats (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 ...) and click Export.

Shortcuts (work from any app; also listed in the menu bar menu):
    Ctrl+Option+Cmd+R    start / stop recording
    Ctrl+Option+Cmd+L    lock the camera where the mouse is / follow again
    Ctrl+Option+Cmd+=    zoom in
    Ctrl+Option+Cmd+-    zoom out

Takes are saved in ~/Movies/Glideshot/Takes, exports in ~/Movies/Glideshot.
Glideshot never connects to the internet. It only records THAT a key was pressed (for
auto-zoom), never which key. No audio yet.

Free and open source: https://github.com/ryanshahine/glideshot
